Message from Ms Tii

A Word from Our Senior Manager

Welcome to the CS Hub — your central place for everything Customer Service. This portal is built for you, by you. Explore the Knowledge Base, stay up to date with announcements, and never miss a training session. Let's make this our go-to resource!

Last month reflected mixed performance across the business. Our General Servicing line delivered a solid 88% service level with a 2.6% abandonment rate, while product lines remained below the 70% target and will require renewed focus going forward. On a positive note, Investments delivered exceptional results, achieving 201% in volume and 193% in value.

Customer experience remains a key priority as we continue to strengthen empathy, consistency, and service excellence in every interaction. A Customer Service Forum will be introduced soon to drive collaboration, shared learning, and continuous improvement.

A reminder that accurate and timely claims submission remains essential, including correct shift capturing and adherence to public holiday guidelines.

Thank you for your continued commitment as we work together to improve performance and elevate the customer experience.

Tiisetso Tsotetsi
SENIOR MANAGER: CUSTOMER SERVICES
